I believe I performed very well this year.

My day-to-day task handling has become stable and efficient. I can debug issues smoothly, and when developing new features, I now approach them with a better mindset—allowing me to identify potential problems earlier than before.

One of the most impactful contributions was the Static Resource Separation project. This change successfully shifted the cost of static resources from being fully covered by BTSE to being billed individually to each white-label. As a result, it significantly reduced BTSE’s overall operational expenses.

On the development side, improving the Email template pipeline was another key achievement. The existing email templates had major rendering issues. After fixing them, developers were able to work with a more modern and intuitive setup. Code reusability was also greatly improved by consolidating previously unclear or inconsistent parameters, which reduced the need for developers to “guess” how the code behaves.

Still related to email, I also built a fully automated translation sync script this year. This removed the need for weekly manual translation updates and significantly reduced the workload and mental burden for the assigned team members.

All of the above work was accompanied by proper documentation, ensuring that the knowledge and experience can be easily shared and reused in the future.

Beyond technical contributions, I had many opportunities to collaborate with different teams this year. Whether discussing feature feasibility with the Intra team or aligning workflows with the App team, I believe my cross-team communication skills were strong. I also actively pushed projects forward, which I feel brought clear value to the team.

Overall, I believe my performance this year was solid and meaningful.
